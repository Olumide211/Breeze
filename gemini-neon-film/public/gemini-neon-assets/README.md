# Gemini 4 Neon: asset pack

Everything needed to prompt the Gemini 4 Neon reveal. Built 18 Nov 2026.

> Status of the name: on 18 Nov 2026, "gemini-4-neon" had been spotted in the Gemini web app code and in Google Cloud's SKU catalog, but Google had not announced it. Post only after Google's announcement.

## What's inside

| folder | contents |
|---|---|
| `logos/` | Gemini spark, full colour (`gemini-spark-color.svg/.png`), white, black; the "Gemini" wordmark in white and black. The pngs are 2048px (spark) and 3000px (wordmark), transparent background |
| `fonts/google-sans/` | Google Sans 400, 500, 600, 700 (latin, woff2) |
| `fonts/google-sans-flex/` | Google Sans Flex 100–900 (latin, woff2) |
| `icons/` | 6 matched white icons, 1024px png + svg, all from Google's Material Symbols Rounded (same weight): 01 deep research, 02 canvas, 03 video, 04 image, 05 live/voice, 06 code |
| `reference/` | Google's official key art for Gemini 4 Argon, Carbon and Hydrogen (16:9), plus `VISUAL-SYSTEM.md`: the colours, layout, element-object pattern, neon colours and a neon realism checklist |
| `sound/` | 10 synthesized sound effects (48kHz wav): neon ignition flicker, neon hum loop, spark zap, glass tink, deep sub hit, soft whoosh, 4 key clicks, send pop, sparkle shimmer, a 12s airy pad |
| `reference/gemini-app/` | your gemini app screenshots: empty box, typed, thinking |
| `preview.png` | everything on one sheet |

## Also inside

- `gemini-neon-prompt.md`: the full prompt for claude code (remotion, 9:16 + 1:1)
- `reference/gemini-app/`: your three gemini app screenshots (home, typed, thinking)

## Optional extras

- 2–3 photos of real neon signs you like, into `reference/`
- a real music bed (the airy pad works as a base)
